*Not on this list: `+y_not_cno+`.* It is not a "`concrete, closable
proof obligation`". The in-source rationale explains why: a rigorous
proof must rule out reaching the argument under _every_ interleaving of
`+beta_reduce+` (which permits reduction under binders and on either
side of an application), requiring an invariant closed under the full
reduction congruence, or a coinductive / step-indexed non-termination
argument. Genuinely out of scope, not merely tedious.
